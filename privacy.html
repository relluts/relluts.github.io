Privacy Policy — Fitters' Fitter

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Fitters' Fitter is an offline-capable industrial pipefitting field reference and calculator application. Your privacy is important to us, and this application is designed to operate without collecting, storing, or transmitting personal user data.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, account credentials, location data, or calculation history to external servers. All pipefitting calculations and inputs remain strictly on your local device.

2. Microphone & Voice Recognition

Fitters' Fitter offers optional voice dictation for entering pipe dimensions and calculator expressions. When you tap the microphone button, audio is processed solely through your device's built-in operating system or browser speech recognition service to convert speech into text inside the input field. Fitters' Fitter does not record, store, or transmit audio files.

3. Offline Storage

The application uses standard browser Service Worker caching solely to store application files locally on your device so the reference charts and calculators work offline in the field.

4. Third-Party Services

Fitters' Fitter does not include third-party advertising, analytics trackers, or data brokers.

5. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support contact listed on the app store page.
